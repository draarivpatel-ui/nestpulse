The service bench for a personal AI NAS

NestPulse sits beside your drives and your local models the way a service panel sits on a well made appliance. Four working planners live on it: capacity math, model fitting, library cleanup, and automation rules. Every number is computed in this tab, every dataset is a labeled demo, and nothing ever leaves the page.

  • Model Fit Quantization ladders against a RAM or VRAM budget

Storage Doctor

Pick your drives and rebuild speed. Every level shows usable space, efficiency, fault tolerance, and how long a rebuild would stretch.

250 MB per second

A TB on the box is not a TiB on the disk, so usable space also shows in TiB. Rebuild estimates assume one spare writing a single stream at the chosen speed, with a 1.25x parity penalty on RAID 5 and 6. Real controllers differ.

Tidy Plans

Demo DatasetThe planner works on a labeled demo library with planted duplicates, burst sets, and aging screenshots. Lower the confidence threshold to see what was hiding.

50 percent or higher

Confidence bands: identical content hashes rank at 100 percent, burst sets at 75 percent, copy suffix patterns at 65 percent, and aged screenshots at 45 percent. The planner reads only this page's manifest, never a real disk.

Demo DatasetNine demo rules run against three days of synthetic hourly sensor logs. The detector flags contradictions, shadowed rules, duplicate effects, and references the logs cannot satisfy, then checks each prediction against what actually fired.

NestPulse

NestPulse is a hackathon concept by Aariv Patel, built for the UGREEN Hackathon 2026 on the Automate Your Workflow track. Every number comes from arithmetic on labeled demo data; no NAS hardware is connected and no vendor relationship is claimed.
