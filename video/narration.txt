NestPulse is a command center for a personal AI NAS, built for the UGREEN Hackathon workflow track. Storage Doctor answers the drive questions first. Enter drive count, size, and rebuild speed, and it computes usable space, efficiency, and rebuild windows for every RAID level, with the math shown on each card. Model Fit checks which open weight models fit your RAM or VRAM budget across seven quantization levels, so you know your ceiling before you download anything. Tidy Plans simulates a cleanup of a photo and file library, ranking duplicate groups and space savings by confidence, and never proposes the same file twice. Rule Forge builds home automation rules and then hunts for trouble, flagging contradictions, shadowed rules, and unknown sensors before you deploy them, and dry runs everything against seventy two hours of synthetic logs. Everything runs locally in your browser, and the datasets are clearly labeled synthetic.
